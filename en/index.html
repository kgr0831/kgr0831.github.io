Problem

Drawing slowed down and strokes became uneven during video calls. I separated the rendering, input and transmission problems.

My role

Five-person team · Whiteboard full-stack
Dec 2025–Jan 2026

Treat rendering, input and transport separately.

Replace the renderer

Moved to PixiJS v8 and WebGPU, with WebGL fallback for unsupported environments.

Improve input and display

Used a One Euro Filter for jitter and Bézier interpolation for smoother strokes.

Reduce transmitted data

Simplified coordinates with Douglas–Peucker and batched updates every 50ms.
